Crypto Mortgages 2026

How Bitcoin can count toward a home loan — and where the limits still are

The short version

In June 2025, FHFA Director Pulte signed a directive ordering Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac to consider crypto as mortgage reserve assets — with no conversion to USD needed. That was a real policy shift. But as of mid-2026 public reporting, implementation is still early and unclear, and lenders decide individually. So: the door is open, but each lender walks through it differently (as of Sept 2026 — verify).

Verify before you apply: mortgage policies change often. Treat everything below as a starting map, then confirm current terms with each lender directly (as of Sept 2026 — verify).

Reserves vs down payment: the distinction that matters

Lenders look at two different buckets, and crypto sits differently in each:

  • Reserves — assets you hold beyond the down payment, proving you can keep paying if income dips. This is what the FHFA directive targets: crypto can be considered here without being sold for dollars first.
  • Down payment — cash delivered at closing. Sellers and settlement agents still expect dollars (or local currency), so BTC used here generally has to be sold first — which is usually a taxable disposal.

Practical takeaway: holding BTC can strengthen a reserves picture, but funding the actual down payment from BTC still means selling (and likely tax). Plan the two buckets separately.

Tax warning: pledging is not selling

  • Pledging BTC as collateral is generally not a sale — so generally no capital-gains event at pledge time.
  • Selling BTC for a down payment generally is taxable — gain or loss measured against your cost basis.
  • Liquidations (forced sale of collateral after a margin call) can also be taxable disposals — another reason to keep buffers conservative.

Rules differ by country and situation. This is a principle, not advice — confirm with a tax professional before you move coins.

How to approach a lender

  • Bring proof of holdings: exchange/custodian statements, wallet attestations — whatever the lender asks for, sourced cleanly.
  • Ask explicitly: "Do you count crypto in reserves? Do you require it custodied a certain way? Seasoned for how long?"
  • Get the down-payment sourcing rules in writing: which funds must be seasoned in a bank account, and for how long.
  • Compare a crypto route against simply selling earlier in a lower-income year — sometimes the boring path costs less in tax.

FAQ

Can Bitcoin count toward a mortgage in 2026?

Possibly as reserves under the FHFA directive — but lenders decide individually, and implementation is still early (as of Sept 2026 — verify).

Do I have to convert BTC to USD first?

For reserves consideration under the directive, no conversion is supposed to be required. For the down payment itself, expect to deliver fiat — meaning a sale somewhere in the chain.

Will pledging BTC trigger capital gains?

Generally no — pledging is not a sale. Selling is. Liquidation of collateral can be a disposal too. Confirm with a tax professional.
