Does Better Accept Bitcoin?

Short answer: no public evidence as of Sept 2026 — not yet

The direct answer

There is no public evidence that Better (the online mortgage lender) accepts Bitcoin — as collateral, in reserves, or otherwise — as of Sept 2026. If that changes, this page will be updated. Until then, assume the answer is no and plan accordingly (as of Sept 2026 — verify).

Could you still use Better as a BTC holder? Possibly — many conventional lenders (Better included) can work with borrowers whose crypto was sold and seasoned as fiat funds. That is a very different thing from a crypto mortgage: expect a taxable sale plus standard fund-sourcing paperwork. Confirm their current rules directly.

FAQ

Does Better accept Bitcoin for mortgages?

No public evidence as of Sept 2026. Check with Better directly for any policy change.

Can I sell BTC and then use Better normally?

Often yes in principle — converted, seasoned fiat funds are standard. But selling is generally taxable, and fund-sourcing rules apply. Confirm both with a tax professional and with Better.
